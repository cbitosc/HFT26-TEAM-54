month, day, hour, minute and second, so a line with minute 61 or month 13 is counted although the
spec says it is invalid. Encode the ranges in the pattern: month 0[1-9]|1[0-2], day
0[1-9]|[12][0-9]|3[01], hour [01][0-9]|2[0-3], minute/second [0-5][0-9], and use [0-9] instead of
\d. (2) "import sys" is unused; ruff reports F401.
